Major Milestones

RECENT ACHIEVEMENTS

  • Church Property Acquisition: Successfully purchased dedicated church property to secure a permanent home for the congregation.
  • Pastoral Team Expansion: Hired an Assistant Pastor to strengthen pastoral care, discipleship, and family counseling.
  • Community Partnerships: Established close collaboration with grassroots organizations, outreach societies, and youth networks across Calgary.
Strategic Vision

WHERE WE ARE HEADED

  • Facility Completion: Finalizing the ongoing building and facility project for enhanced community use.
  • Pastoral Visitation: Reconnecting with inactive members through systematic house-to-house visitations and personal care.
  • Investing in Next-Gen: Launching life skills sharing workshops and youth leadership capacity programs.
  • Women’s Ministry Empowerment: Expanding targeted empowerment initiatives and mentorship for women.
Foundations

VISION & CALLING

ራዕያችን፣ ተልዕኮአችን እና ዋናው ግባችን

01 / Vision

PROCLAIM & DISCIPLE

ራዕያችን

To proclaim the saving gospel of Jesus Christ to all people, leading them into salvation and nurturing them into committed disciples of Christ.

ሰዎች ሁሉ የጠፉ በመሆኑ የክርስቶስን ወንጌል በመስበክ ወደ ደህንነት መጥራት እና ደቀመዝሙር እንዲሆኑ ማድረግ ነው።

02 / Mission

EQUIP & MOBILIZE

ተልዕኮአችን

Teaching the Word to all believers, empowering them for ministry, and mobilizing them as effective workers in the harvest field.

አምነው የዳኑትን ሁሉ ወንጌልን ማስተማር፣ ወደ አገልጋይነት ማብቃት እና ተመልሰው የወንጌል ሰራተኛ እንዲሆኑ ማድረግ።

03 / Purpose

CHRISTLIKENESS

ግባችን

Maturing believers into the character and likeness of Christ, preparing them as heirs of the everlasting Kingdom.

እግዚአብሔርን ወደመምሰል እና የልጁን መንግስት ወራሽ መሆን ወደሚችሉበት መጨረሻ ማድረስ ነው።
